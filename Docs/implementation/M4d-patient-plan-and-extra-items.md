## Behaviour change

**1. Plan for the coming weeks** (new section on the follow-up form):
- There's one folding block per remaining week; the next week is open.
- Each block starts from the plan saved earlier or, the first time, from the package.
- The doctor can change quantity, unit and notes, remove items, and **add** an injection or vitamin from the lists, or a drug typed by name.
- The plan is saved with the follow-up, and editing a week saves it again.
- It belongs to **this patient**: the package never changes.
- The enrollment page shows what's planned under each week that isn't recorded yet.

**2. *Given this week* starts from the plan.**
- Once a plan exists, the next week's form lists that week's planned items **ticked** (decision 2), with the planned quantity. The doctor unticks what wasn't given.
- Without a plan, the package's items for the week are listed, also ticked.
- The package's other items stay below, unticked, for anything given early or late.
- **Add an item given this week**: from the injection or vitamin lists, or typed by name.

**3. Items outside the package.**
- They're recorded with their quantity, unit and notes. They're **not charged** (decision 1).
- The follow-up page marks them *Outside the package*. Editing a week shows them again.

**4. Checks on the server.** Each given or planned line names **exactly one** item: a package item of this enrollment, an injection, a vitamin, or a typed name. The server refuses, with a message:
- a line that names nothing, or two items;
- a quantity of zero or less;
- an unknown injection or vitamin;
- a typed name over 200 characters;
- a plan for a week that isn't still to come.

The database enforces the one-item rule too.

**5. Kept across a language switch:** the plan, and anything added this week.

## Unchanged

- Four weeks.
- Week 4 completes the enrollment.
- Only the latest week can be deleted.
- Lab results.
- Who can record.
- Prices and balances.
- Packages.

## Database change (migration `PatientPlanAndExtraItems`)

| Operation | Detail |
|---|---|
| CreateTable `EnrollmentPlannedItems` | Enrollment, week (1–4), exactly one of package item, injection, vitamin or typed name, quantity (> 0), unit, notes, created at and by. Check constraints `CK_EnrollmentPlannedItems_OneItem`, `_Week`, `_Quantity`. Foreign keys **Restrict**: history is never deleted by cascade. Indexes on enrollment+week, package item, injection and vitamin |
| `WeeklyAdministeredItems` | `PackageItemId` becomes optional. New optional `InjectionId` and `VitaminId` (foreign keys, Restrict, indexed), `CustomName` (200) and `Unit` (50). Check constraint `CK_WeeklyAdministeredItems_OneItem`; every existing row names a package item, so it passes |
